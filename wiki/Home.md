# GhostlyShare User Guide

GhostlyShare finds local web apps and lets you share them either on your local network or through a temporary public Cloudflare link.

The desktop app is the easiest starting point. The `ghs` CLI offers the same core workflows for terminals, servers, and scripts.

**Current release:** `1.3.0.0`
